## 1. Registry Architecture & Data Governance

The repository manages data across five strictly isolated directory tiers under `data/`:
```text
data/
├── raw/        # Pristine, immutable source downloads (SAR scenes, raw AIS CSVs, GRIB/NetCDF metocean files)
├── interim/    # Intermediate stages (cropped GeoTIFFs, cleaned AIS tracks, resampled velocity grids)
├── processed/  # Standardized analysis-ready artifacts (GeoJSON polygons, feature vectors, probability rasters)
├── synthetic/  # Deterministic test cases with known mathematical ground truth for unit/adversarial tests
└── metadata/   # Registries, schema definitions, and cases.csv
```

### Data Integrity Rules:
1. **Zero Fabrication:** Never synthesize or fake data without explicitly tagging it with `provenance: "SYNTHETIC"` and `is_ground_truth: false`.
2. **Immutability:** Raw data in `data/raw/` must never be altered in-place. All transformations write to `interim/` or `processed/`.
3. **Reproducibility:** Every processed artifact must contain a JSON metadata sidecar documenting the upstream source file, software version, transformation parameters, and timestamp.

## 2. Satellite Remote Sensing Datasets

### 2.1 Copernicus Sentinel-1 Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR)
- **Dataset Name:** Sentinel-1 Level-1 Ground Range Detected (GRD) Interferometric Wide (IW) Swath
- **Provider:** European Space Agency (ESA) / Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem (CDSE) / Alaska Satellite Facility (ASF) DAAC
- **URL:** [Copernicus Browser](https://browser.dataprocess.copernicus.eu/) / [ASF Search API](https://asf.alaska.edu/)
- **Geographic Coverage:** Global oceans, coastal zones, and shipping corridors
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2014–Present (Sentinel-1A operational; Sentinel-1C launched Dec 2024)
- **Spatial Resolution:** $10\text{ m} \times 10\text{ m}$ pixel spacing (IW mode)
- **Temporal Resolution:** 6 to 12-day repeat cycle per pass
- **Format:** GeoTIFF (converted from SAFE format) / NetCDF
- **Polarizations:** Dual polarization: VV (Vertical transmit/Vertical receive) + VH (Vertical transmit/Horizontal receive)
- **License:** Open Access (Copernicus Legal Notice — free for scientific and commercial use)
- **Access Requirements:** Free registered account on Copernicus CDSE or Microsoft Planetary Computer STAC API
- **Quality & Artifacts:** Orthorectified and radiometrically calibrated ($\sigma^0$ backscatter in decibels). Speckle noise filtered via Lee/Frost or adaptive median filtering.
- **Intended Use:** Detection of oil slick boundaries by measuring capillary wave dampening (specular reflection causing dark pixel signatures relative to ambient sea clutter).
- **Known Limitations:**
  - Look-alikes produced by wind speeds $< 3\text{ m/s}$ (mirror-calm water) or natural biogenic surfactant films.
  - Slicks disperse or become undetectable by SAR at wind speeds $> 12\text{--}14\text{ m/s}$.

### 2.2 Copernicus Sentinel-2 Multi-Spectral Instrument (MSI)
- **Dataset Name:** Sentinel-2 Level-2A Bottom-Of-Atmosphere (BOA) Reflectance
- **Provider:** ESA / Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem
- **URL:** [Copernicus Data Space](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/)
- **Geographic Coverage:** Global coastal waters within 20 km of shore
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2015–Present
- **Spatial Resolution:** 10m (Bands 2, 3, 4, 8) / 20m (Red-edge and SWIR bands)
- **Temporal Resolution:** 5-day repeat cycle
- **Format:** GeoTIFF (Level-2A SAFE format)
- **License:** Open Access (Copernicus terms)
- **Intended Use:** Look-alike discrimination and optical verification under cloud-free daylight conditions. Chlorophyll absorption (algal bloom look-alike detection) and Sunglint enhancement of thick oil emulsions.
- **Known Limitations:** Completely obstructed by cloud cover; ineffective during nighttime.

### 2.3 Oil Spill Detection Benchmark Dataset (OSDD)
- **Dataset Name:** Sentinel-1 SAR Oil Spill Detection Dataset
- **Provider:** Research community (Kshitij / Roboflow / Zenodo Open Access)
- **URL:** [Zenodo / Kaggle Oil Spill Detection Repositories](https://www.kaggle.com/)
- **Geographic Coverage:** Mediterranean Sea, North Sea, Gulf of Mexico
- **Spatial Resolution:** Patches of $256 \times 256$ to $1250 \times 650$ pixels at native Sentinel-1 resolution
- **Format:** PNG / GeoTIFF with paired binary segmentation masks
- **License:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC-BY 4.0)
- **Intended Use:** Training, validating, and testing the `ThresholdDetector` and `SegmentationModel` dark-spot extraction algorithms.
- **Known Limitations:** Mask labels generated by human analysts, with occasional ambiguity between low-wind look-alikes and mineral oil.

## 3. Oceanographic & Atmospheric Environmental Datasets

### 3.1 ECMWF ERA5 Atmospheric Reanalysis
- **Dataset Name:** ERA5 Hourly Data on Single Levels from 1940 to Present
- **Provider:** European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts (ECMWF) / Copernicus Climate Change Service (C3S)
- **URL:** [Copernicus Climate Data Store (CDS)](https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/)
- **Geographic Coverage:** Global ($0.25^\circ \times 0.25^\circ$ regular latitude/longitude grid, $\approx 31\text{ km}$)
- **Temporal Coverage:** 1940–Present, hourly intervals
- **Key Variables:**
  - `u10`: 10-meter eastward wind component ($\text{m/s}$)
  - `v10`: 10-meter northward wind component ($\text{m/s}$)
  - `msl`: Mean sea level pressure ($\text{Pa}$)
- **Format:** GRIB / NetCDF-4
- **License:** Open Access (Copernicus license)
- **Access Requirements:** Free CDS API token (`~/.cdsapirc`)
- **Intended Use:** Driving the wind advection component ($3.0\%\text{--}3.5\%$ wind drag factor) in backward and forward Lagrangian drift modeling, and computing look-alike risk thresholds ($u_{10} < 3\text{ m/s}$).
- **Known Limitations:** Coarse spatial resolution ($31\text{ km}$) may smooth localized coastal micro-climates and land-sea breeze dynamics.

### 3.2 Copernicus Marine Service (CMEMS) Global Ocean Physics
- **Dataset Name:** Global Ocean Physics Analysis and Forecast (`GLOBAL_ANALYSISFORECAST_PHY_001_024`) / GLORYS12 Reanalysis
- **Provider:** Copernicus Marine Environment Monitoring Service (CMEMS) / Mercator Ocean International
- **URL:** [Copernicus Marine Data Store](https://marine.copernicus.eu/)
- **Geographic Coverage:** Global Ocean ($1/12^\circ \approx 8\text{ km}$ horizontal resolution, 50 vertical levels)
- **Temporal Coverage:** Hourly surface fields, daily 3D fields; 1993–Present
- **Key Variables:**
  - `uo`: Surface eastward ocean current velocity ($\text{m/s}$)
  - `vo`: Surface northward ocean current velocity ($\text{m/s}$)
  - `zos`: Sea surface height above geoid ($\text{m}$)
  - `tob`: Sea surface temperature ($^\circ\text{C}$)
- **Format:** NetCDF-4 (CF-1.6 compliant)
- **License:** Free and open access under Copernicus Marine terms
- **Access Requirements:** Free CMEMS user credentials via `copernicusmarine` Python client
- **Intended Use:** Direct hydrodynamic forcing for the surface advective drift velocity ($\vec{u}_c$) in the Lagrangian trajectory engine.
- **Known Limitations:** Does not resolve sub-mesoscale turbulence $< 8\text{ km}$ or shallow nearshore surf-zone wave breaking.

## 4. Vessel Tracking & AIS Datasets

### 4.1 NOAA MarineCadastre AIS
- **Dataset Name:** MarineCadastre Vessel Transit AIS Records
- **Provider:** Bureau of Ocean Energy Management (BOEM) & National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA)
- **URL:** [MarineCadastre.gov AIS](https://marinecadastre.gov/ais/)
- **Geographic Coverage:** US Coastal Waters and Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ)
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2009–Present (Daily archives at 1-minute filtered intervals)
- **Key Variables:** `MMSI`, `BaseDateTime`, `LAT`, `LON`, `SOG` (Speed Over Ground), `COG` (Course Over Ground), `Heading`, `VesselName`, `IMO`, `CallSign`, `VesselType`, `Status`, `Draft`
- **Format:** CSV / GeoPackage
- **License:** US Government Public Domain (Free open access)
- **Intended Use:** Benchmark vessel traffic analysis for the Huntington Beach case and rigorous verification of AIS gap-analysis and kinematic interpolation algorithms.

### 4.2 Danish Maritime Authority (DMA) Open AIS
- **Dataset Name:** DMA Historical AIS Data
- **Provider:** Danish Maritime Authority
- **URL:** [DMA AIS Data](https://dma.dk/)
- **Geographic Coverage:** North Sea, Baltic Sea, Kattegat/Skagerrak shipping straits
- **Temporal Coverage:** 2006–Present (Continuous daily CSV archives)
- **Format:** CSV / zipped text files
- **License:** Open Government Data
- **Intended Use:** Benchmarking high-density maritime traffic corridors with complex vessel crossing patterns.

### 4.3 Global Fishing Watch (GFW) Public AIS
- **Dataset Name:** Public Vessel Trajectory & Anchor Event Feeds
- **Provider:** Global Fishing Watch
- **URL:** [Global Fishing Watch Research Datasets](https://globalfishingwatch.org/datasets-and-code/)
- **Format:** BigQuery public datasets / CSV
- **License:** Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
