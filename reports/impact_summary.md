# Executive Impact Summary — SIH26143

## 1. Operational Value for NTRO & Maritime Agencies
1. **National Security & Maritime Domain Awareness (MDA):**
   - Enables continuous, automated surveillance across India's 2.37 million km² Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ).
   - Eliminates manual correlation delays between Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) / Copernicus satellite feeds and Directorate General of Shipping / Indian Coast Guard AIS networks.
2. **Legal Defensibility & Evidence Integrity:**
   - Overcomes the primary bottleneck in prosecuting international maritime polluters: lack of admissible physical proof.
   - By subjecting candidate hypotheses to adversarial falsification and counterfactual hydrodynamic verification, investigators obtain mathematically defensible attribution packages that withstand scrutiny in international maritime courts (ITLOS / Admiralty Courts).
3. **Resource Optimization via Active Sensing:**
   - Maritime patrol aircraft (Dornier 228 / P-8I Neptune) and satellite tasking are expensive operational resources.
   - Algorithmic Expected Information Gain ensures reconnaissance sorties are directed specifically to spatial sectors where ambiguity between competing hypotheses is highest, cutting wasted flight hours.
4. **Mitigation of False Accusations:**
   - The principled `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` abstention gate prevents diplomatic incidents and erroneous detentions resulting from simplistic spatial proximity heuristics.
